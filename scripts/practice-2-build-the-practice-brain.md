# Script 2: Build the Practice Brain

1. I know it sounds weird, but how your practice runs usually lives in one place — your head.

2. So every pricing decision, every policy you've got to weigh in on, every scheduling question waits on you, even mid-session. And to be honest, that's probably dragging your bandwidth into the gutter.

3. In 30 days, we move it into a Practice Brain — services, pricing, policies, workflows, who does what. We squeeze the lemon to get that juice.

4. Once it's in there, it's private, it's yours, and your front desk can ask it anything.

5. Your team gets your answer, in your words, without interrupting you. One source of truth for every workflow.

6. Less stress, fewer interruptions, and a practice that runs with real cohesiveness — the way you'd run it yourself.
