# Script 2: Build the Brain

1. I know it sounds weird, but how your business runs usually lives in one place — your head. Your pricing, your rules, how you handle callbacks, which jobs you take and which ones you don't.

2. So every question waits on you, even when you're up on the roof or under a house. And to be honest, that's probably dragging your bandwidth into the gutter. You're the bottleneck, and you didn't sign up for that.

3. In 30 days, we move it into a Company Brain — your pricing, your rules, your job SOPs, who does what, and your scripts. We squeeze the lemon to get that juice, and once it's in there, it's not going anywhere.

4. It's private, it's yours, and your whole team can ask it questions. Your office manager stops bringing you every call. They ask the Brain first and get the answer in your words.

5. One source of truth for how your business runs. That means less training time, fewer mistakes, and a business that actually operates the way you want it to — even when you're not there.

6. Fewer calls to the owner, real consistency across your crew, and a business that's easier to scale and easier to sell. That's what a brain does.
