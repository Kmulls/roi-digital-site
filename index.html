The Blueprint Assessment

Which Build Stage Are You In?

Eight questions, two minutes. Find the structural problem in your business — and the first renovation move.

STAGE 01

Overloaded Foundation

“Everything runs through me.”

The whole building sits on one person’s shoulders. Revenue is real, but the foundation cracks the moment you step away. First move: separate your energy from the structure.

STAGE 02

Missing Rooms

“I don’t know who to hire.”

Half the rooms in your building are empty or unfinished. Every hire feels like a clone of you. First move: write the role around the rooms you don’t have, not the ones you do.

STAGE 03

Crossed Wires

“My team isn’t in sync.”

Good people, but the wiring between rooms is tangled. Decisions stall, signals cross. First move: map who owns which decisions and where two names show up for the same one.

STAGE 04

Wrong Front Door

“Working hard, wrong results.”

You’re pushing clients through a side entrance instead of the front door. The effort is high, the return is thin. First move: look at how your last five best clients actually found you.
