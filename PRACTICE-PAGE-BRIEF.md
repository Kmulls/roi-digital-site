## The leaks
- Eyebrow: The problem
- Headline: You don't have a demand problem. / You have a front-desk problem.
- Intro: People are already reaching out. The gaps are in what happens next.
- Cards (title + body):
  1. The call nobody answered: A new client calls about a treatment at 6:40pm and gets voicemail. She books with the practice that picked up.
  2. The inquiry that sat overnight: A consult request or intake form waits days for a reply.
  3. The empty chair: No-shows and late cancels, with no waitlist to fill them. Every open hour is revenue you can't get back.
  4. The client who drifted: Treatments are due every few months, and nobody sends the reminder.
  5. The owner as help desk: Pricing, insurance and scheduling questions pull you out of sessions.
- Closing line: Underneath it all, the practice runs through you. The protocols, pricing and policies live in your head, so your front desk can't move without you.

## Phase 1 · Days 1–30
- Headline: Your Practice Brain. / Your team asks it, not you.
- Body: We get your protocols, policies and know-how out of your head and into one private system your front desk can ask.
- Bullets:
  - Services and pricing: treatments, packages, what's included, what you never discount
  - Policies: cancellations, deposits, intake requirements, insurance and self-pay rules
  - Who does what: front desk, providers, clinicians, who approves what
  - What we say: replies, reminders and scripts, written in your voice and reviewed by you

## Phase 2 · Days 31–90
- Headline: Your front-desk OS. / It runs while you're with clients.
- Items:
  - AI receptionist + missed-call text-back: every caller greeted, answered and offered a time, 24/7
  - Instant inquiry responder: every web form and DM answered in minutes, not days
  - Smart scheduling + waitlist: cancellations filled from the waitlist automatically
  - Reminders and rebooking: fewer no-shows, and clients reminded when treatments are due
  - Reviews: a review request after every visit
  - Monday owner brief: one page of inquiries, bookings, no-shows, open hours and revenue

## Built for healthcare (new section, place after Phase 2)
- Headline: Your clients' privacy comes first. / So does their safety.
- Bullets:
  - Privacy by design. We build only on tools that sign a Business Associate Agreement with your practice.
  - The AI never gives clinical advice. It handles logistics: hours, pricing, availability and intake steps. Anything clinical goes to a person.
  - Urgent calls go to people, fast. If a caller sounds like they're in crisis, they're given 988 and 911 right away and your on-call provider is alerted.
  - You approve every word. No message goes out that you haven't reviewed.
  - You own your data. It lives in your accounts, under your policies.
